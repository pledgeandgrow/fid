# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to every space the Fides project runs: the
compiler and website repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, GitHub
Discussions, and any event or channel the project operates. It also applies
when someone represents the project in public.

## The standard

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

Behaviour that contributes to a healthy project:

- Being direct and kind at the same time. Technical disagreement is welcome;
  contempt is not.
- Critiquing the design, the code, or the argument, never the person.
- Assuming competence and good faith, and asking before concluding.
- Accepting a decision once it is made, while keeping the door open to
  revisiting it with new evidence.
- Remembering that most contributors are volunteers, and that "no" is a
  complete answer to a feature request.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including sexualised
  language or imagery and unwelcome attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or events.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting on this repository (it reaches only maintainers, and is the
project's private channel for conduct reports as well) or by asking any
maintainer for a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially; the
reporter is never named without consent.

Maintainers will respond to every report, and may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this code, or
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. A maintainer accused in a
report does not take part in handling it.
