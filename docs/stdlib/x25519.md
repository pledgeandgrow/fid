## x25519_scalarmult

X25519(k, u) of RFC 7748 §5: the u-coordinate of [clamp(k)] u.
Both arguments are 32 bytes; bit 255 of u is ignored and a
non-canonical u (>= p) is reduced, as the RFC requires. Panics on a
wrong length. Constant time in k (a fixed 255-step Montgomery ladder
with masked swaps).
