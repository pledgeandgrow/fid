## tls_alpn_wire

A comma-separated list of protocol names ("h2,http/1.1") in ALPN's wire
format. Spaces around a name are dropped; an empty name, or one longer
than 255 bytes, is left out. Empty when no name is left.

## tls_alpn_choose

The server's choice (RFC 7301 3.2): the first of the server's names
(`server`, wire format) that the client also offers (`client`, wire
format). Returns the offset of that entry's length byte in `server`, or
-1 when they share none or a list is malformed.
