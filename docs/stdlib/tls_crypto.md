## x25519

X25519: scalar(32B, clamped internally) × point(32B) → 32B shared.
`scalar` and `point` are little-endian Vec<i64> of 32 bytes. The ladder
is x25519.fd's, constant time in the scalar with bit 255 of the point
ignored; this module's own BigInt ladder read that bit and swapped on
a branch, and got RFC 7748's second vector wrong (BG216).
